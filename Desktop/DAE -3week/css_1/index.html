What is ESL?

ESL stands for English as a Second Language. It refers to the teaching and learning of English by non-native speakers. ESL programs are designed to help individuals improve their English language skills for various purposes, such as communication, education, and employment.

Benefits of ESL Learning

  • Improved communication skills
  • Better job opportunities
  • Enhanced cultural understanding
  • Increased confidence in using English
