Who We Are

Our Mission

Our mission is to address the shortage of ESL tutors in New Haven by promoting the importance of ESL education and connecting learners with local bilingual support.

📖

Our Story

I was once an ESL learner in New Haven. Though I graduated from the program, I continued to face challenges with English. With support from New Haven Reads, I improved my literacy skills and began helping others — including translating lessons for new students.

Experiencing firsthand the difficulties families face when finding available ESL programs inspired me to create this website and recruit bilingual tutors to support our community.

💬

What is ESL?

ESL stands for English as a Second Language. It refers to the teaching and learning of English by non-native speakers. ESL programs help individuals improve their English for communication, education, and employment.

Benefits of ESL Learning

  • Improved communication skills
  • Better job opportunities
  • Enhanced cultural understanding
  • Increased confidence in English

Cultural Connections

ESL students are connected with tutors who understand their language, culture, and unique learning experience.

By creating opportunities for ESL programs to recruit bilingual tutors, this project helps reduce language barriers and supports ESL learners throughout the New Haven community.

The Data

Research Findings

This is a statewide — and nationwide — issue.

Several school districts are impacted by the increasing demand for tutors as ESL learner populations grow. There is a rapidly increasing number of immigrants arriving in New Haven, CT from Latin America and African countries.

Texas

60,000

Students needing tutors — up from an estimated 1,000 pre-pandemic

"Before the pandemic, four local organizations served the tutoring needs of an estimated 1,000 students. Now we're facing numbers in the 50,000 to 60,000 range — five times more than what we were planning even six months ago."

— Derek Little, Deputy Chief of Teaching and Learning

California

64%

Of 22,000 students in Hacienda La Puente USD are ESL learners

"In the Hacienda La Puente Unified School District, 64% of the 22,000-student population are ESL learners."

— Wendy Maestas-Padilla
